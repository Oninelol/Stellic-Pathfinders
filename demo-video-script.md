# Flightplan — 2-minute demo video
**Concept: "Six tabs vs. one pigeon"** — chaos cold-open (20s), then the pigeon takes over as narrator and gives the tour.

One narrator voice the whole way through — but after the wipe, the narrator *is* the pigeon. Record VO last, after picture-lock. Total VO ≈ 275 words ≈ safely under 2:00 at a relaxed pace.

---

## Beat sheet + narration + what's on screen

### BEAT 1 — The problem (0:00–0:18)
**Screen:** Fast cuts, ~2s each, slightly punched-in and stressy: SIO/course registration page → a degree audit PDF → Handshake → a goals list in Notes → an OIE email about I-20 renewal → Google Calendar. (Screen-record real pages or convincing stand-ins; add a subtle camera shake + tab-switch *click* sounds.)

**VO:**
> "Planning college takes six tabs. One for courses. One for your degree audit. One for jobs. One for goals you wrote in October and never opened again. And if you're an international student — add two more. Nobody's keeping it all together."

*(beat of silence, all tabs slam shut)*

### BEAT 2 — The wipe (0:18–0:26)
**Screen:** The pigeon (excited flap frames) flies across the frame left-to-right and wipes to the Flightplan login page → sign-in.

**VO:**
> "Hi. I'm a pigeon. I keep it together. This is **Flightplan** — your degree and your career, on one runway."

### BEAT 3 — The quiz becomes your homepage (0:26–0:48)
**Screen:** Onboarding quiz: pick "Arts & Design," type 3 real goals ("apply to 3 internships," "find an on-campus job," "GPA 4.0"), answer the international-student question **Yes** → Start → land on Home, where those exact goals are now pinned and ranked. Slow the moment the typed goal appears on the homepage — that's the money shot.

**VO:**
> "When you first land, I ask what you're chasing this semester. Whatever you type doesn't disappear into a form — it *becomes* your homepage. Your goals, your five courses, your week — one glance."

### BEAT 4 — Home tour + I nag you, lovingly (0:48–1:10)
**Screen:** Cursor sweeps Home: 5 colorful course chips, the Sun–Sat schedule grid, a rust hover-tip appearing ("Leads to your course map"). Then click the pigeon → reminder bubble opens with color-coded GOALS / TO-DO / VISA chips → check a task → pigeon plays the excited flap. Then click ✕ with tasks left → pigeon plays angry/crying. (Let both reactions breathe — judges will smile.)

**VO:**
> "I keep a list of what actually needs you — goals, to-dos, visa dates. Check something off, I celebrate. Close me with work left… I have feelings about that."

### BEAT 5 — Planner + Career (1:10–1:30)
**Screen:** Planner: drag a course to another semester; hover to reveal prerequisite arrows; show a red out-of-order course. Cut to Career tab: scroll the job list, bookmark one, open a student start-up card, then one quick shot of the resume editor + Export PDF.

**VO:**
> "Drag a course and I recheck your prerequisites live — red means we have a problem. Your career plan lives next door: real listings, student start-ups, and a resume that exports ready to send."

### BEAT 6 — The heart (1:30–1:52)
**Screen:** Personal goals page: drag a widget, doodle a quick star with the pen, tick a habit → star drops into the jar (her hand-drawn animation), open the letter-to-future-self and seal it. Then the kicker: quiz answered "No — domestic student" → the Visa tab vanishes from the nav (before/after side-by-side, 2s).

**VO:**
> "There's also a page that's just *yours* — habits that drop stars in a jar, a letter your future self opens at graduation. And Flightplan reshapes itself to you: domestic students never see a visa tab. International students get a whole flight crew."

### BEAT 7 — Landing (1:52–2:00)
**Screen:** Pigeon standing proud on its planet, zoom out to the logo. Team names. "Flightplan — degree + career, one runway."

**VO:**
> "Flightplan. Chart it, chase it, land it."

## Shooting checklist (do these in order)
1. **Record all screen footage first** from `flightplan-preview.html` (offline — zero loading hiccups) in a full-screen browser window at **1920×1080**, macOS screen recording or QuickTime. Hide bookmarks bar.
2. Do each beat as its own recording — short takes are easier to cut than one long one.
3. Mouse discipline: move slow and deliberate; pause 1s before/after every click (gives you edit handles).
4. Do the **quiz beat twice** — once answering Yes (international), once No (domestic) — so you have the before/after for Beat 6.
5. For pigeon overlay reactions you can also screen-record the pigeon big (zoom the browser to 200% on the corner widget) to get clean crops.
6. **Cut picture first, record VO after** — read against the locked cut. ~150 words/min; if you're racing, cut words not pace.
7. Music: one lo-fi/acoustic track, duck it -12dB under VO. Add tiny SFX only on: tab clicks (Beat 1), the wipe whoosh, the star dropping, the letter seal.
8. Export: H.264, 1920×1080, ~16 Mbps. Watch it once on your phone before submitting.
